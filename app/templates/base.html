About InternMatch

InternMatch is an AI-powered internship recommendation and skill gap analysis platform designed for computer science and engineering students. It matches candidate resumes to technical opportunities using Sentence-BERT embeddings, canonical skill taxonomies, and explainable multi-factor scoring.
