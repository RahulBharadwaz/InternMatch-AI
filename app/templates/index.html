Frequently Asked Questions

What is InternMatch?
InternMatch is an AI-powered internship recommendation and skill gap analysis platform designed to help computer science and engineering students discover technical roles aligned with their genuine skills and career trajectory.
How does the hybrid recommendation algorithm work?
InternMatch combines rule-based skill verification, Sentence-BERT contextual text embeddings, multi-document TF-IDF lexical matching, education eligibility verification, and student role preferences into an explainable composite match score.
How does skill gap analysis help me?
For every recommended internship, InternMatch breaks down matched competencies vs. missing requirements and suggests targeted learning resources (courses, documentation, tutorials) so you can proactively prepare before applying.
