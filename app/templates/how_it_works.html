How InternMatch Works

InternMatch operates an end-to-end Machine Learning and Natural Language Processing pipeline designed to convert unstructured resume text and student preferences into calibrated, explainable internship recommendations.

Step 1: Student Profile & Preferences

Create your candidate profile with your institution, academic qualification (e.g. B.Tech in Computer Science & AI), preferred engineering domains (AI/ML, Backend, Cloud, Full-Stack), target job roles, and relocation preferences.

Step 2: Privacy-Preserving Resume Ingestion

Upload your resume in PDF, DOCX, or TXT format. The system uses PyMuPDF and python-docx to extract text, followed by automated PII redaction to mask sensitive contact details (such as phone numbers and emails) prior to algorithmic processing and persistence.

Step 3: Canonical Skill Extraction & Normalization

Raw text is processed by a rule-based canonical skill extractor covering 6 specialized domains (Programming Languages, Frameworks, Cloud, Databases, Machine Learning, Soft Skills). Boundary-safe regular expressions guarantee that subwords (e.g., Java inside JavaScript) do not trigger erroneous extractions.

Step 4: Sentence-BERT Contextual Semantic Encoding

To understand resume semantics beyond exact keywords, InternMatch uses the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 Sentence Transformer. The model projects resumes and opportunity descriptions into a 384-dimensional dense vector space and computes cosine similarities:

Cosine_Similarity(u, v) = (u • v) / (||u|| * ||v||)

An in-memory embedding cache ensures that pre-computed opportunity vectors deliver sub-500ms inference response times.

Step 5: Explainable Multi-Factor Scoring

The final ranking score is computed via a transparent, weighted linear model balancing technical and contextual factors:

  • Skill Overlap (30%): Jaccard & canonical match against required technical proficiencies.
  • Semantic Similarity (25%): Dense contextual alignment from Sentence-BERT.
  • Lexical Text Similarity (15%): Multi-document TF-IDF cosine similarity baseline.
  • Education Compatibility (15%): Academic qualification matching (B.Tech, M.Tech, CGPA).
  • Domain & Role Preference (10%): Alignment with candidate's stated career targets.
  • Location Preference (5%): Proximity match or relocation willingness.

Step 6: Skill Gap Analysis & Learning Roadmap

For every recommended role, InternMatch provides transparent explainability: why the match occurred, which candidate skills matched, and which skills are missing. Each missing skill links to verified, curated learning resources to help students upskill.

Step 7: Application Tracking

Students can bookmark opportunities, follow direct application links, and update their application status across stages (Applied, Under Review, Shortlisted, Interview Scheduled, Offer Extended).
